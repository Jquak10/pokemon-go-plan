SOURCE PRECEDENCE

When sources disagree

1
Explicit official Pokémon GO schedule Highest priority for published event, raid, and temporary-limit information.
2
Official replacement / suppression notice Hides conflicting normal seasonal schedules during an explicitly published override window.
3
GO Calendar General event and recurring schedule coverage where no official override supersedes it.
4
Do not invent availability If a reliable source does not establish an event, the planner does not create one merely to fill a gap.
✓ Official Pokémon GO

Official announcements

Explicit event schedules, special raid rotations, temporary Remote Raid limits, and official notices that replace normal seasonal schedules.

Role: authoritative scheduling source
GO Calendar

General event calendar

Broad event coverage and recurring schedule information. Official supplements take precedence when the same event is published directly.
