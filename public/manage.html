Current raid recommendations

These recommendations combine the Pokémon's general internet/meta value with your own targets, progress, completion status and preferences.

How recommendations work

Internet value = Is this Pokémon generally worth raiding?

Personal value = Do you personally still need it?

Your recommendation = A combination of both.

Add or update a target

Targets tell the recommendation engine what you personally want to achieve for a Pokémon.

Your target does not replace the general raid-value assessment. Instead, the two are combined to decide how strongly the raid should be recommended to you.

Example

A very strong raid Pokémon may still be recommended even if you have completed your personal target.

A weaker Pokémon may become more important to you if you have a large unfinished Mega Energy, Candy XL or raid target.
