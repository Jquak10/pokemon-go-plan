Privacy, recovery & support

What the Pokémon GO Battle Planner stores, how its private links work, how backups and deletion behave, and how to report a problem safely.

No account required
THE SHORT VERSION

Your private links are your credentials

The planner does not use an email/password account. Your management URL is a bearer credential with read/write access to your planner, and your calendar subscription URL is a bearer credential for a read-only calendar feed. Anyone who has one of those links can use the access it grants, so keep both private.

Never post a management URL, calendar URL, Admin key, or Planner Backup in a public issue, screenshot, chat, or support request.

Planner data

What the planner stores

Planner-owned data in D1 includes your timezone, selected event sources, recommendation weights and paid-battle preferences, Targets and Target notes, battle history, Remote usage, Max Particle/resource state, temporary per-day planning overrides, and any saved Max tier/cost overrides.

The service also stores hashed or generated credential state needed to validate management and calendar capabilities. Public event, Pokémon, ranking, and source- health data is stored separately from your personal planner.

Local-only

What is not part of your planner record

There is no email/password account and the planner does not maintain a full copy of your Pokémon storage or battle team roster. The System / Light / Dark appearance choice is saved only in this browser's local storage, not in your D1 planner record.

Downloading a Planner Backup creates a JSON file for you. The planner does not keep that downloaded file as a separate server-side recovery archive.

Private access

Management and calendar links

The management URL can read and change the planner. If it may have been exposed, open Preferences and rotate it; the old management URL stops working immediately.

Calendar URLs are read-only bearer credentials. Signed calendar URLs can be regenerated or revoked independently. Rotating the management link does not rotate calendar credentials, and rotating a calendar URL does not replace the management link.

Backup & recovery

Backups are your recovery path

A Planner Backup contains preferences, Targets, notes, battle/resource history, and related planner state. It does not contain your management link, calendar links, or credential hashes, but it can still contain private notes and gameplay history.

If you lose the management link, recovery requires a previously saved Planner Backup: create a new planner, save its new management link, then restore the backup into that new empty planner. Without the management link or a saved backup, the old planner cannot be recovered.

Deletion

Permanent planner deletion

Preferences → Delete Planner permanently removes that planner's stored Targets, notes, battle/resource history, settings, and private calendar credential state. Its management and calendar links stop working. The deletion cannot be undone.

A backup file you already downloaded is on your own device or storage provider; deleting the server-side planner does not delete copies you kept elsewhere.

Support

Report incorrect data or an app problem

Use the public GitHub issue tracker for incorrect Pokémon/form/event data, broken behavior, or other application defects. Describe what you expected, what happened, and—when relevant—the Pokémon/form, event name, date/time, timezone, browser/device, and a redacted screenshot.

Do not include private management URLs, calendar subscription URLs, Admin keys, Planner Backup files, or screenshots that reveal those values.
